Privacy Policy

How D.A. BluePrint Technologies collects, uses, and protects information.

Our Commitment to Privacy

D.A. BluePrint Technologies is committed to protecting the privacy of our clients, users, and website visitors. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you use our platform or contact us.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact information provided through our inquiry form (name, email, phone, organization)
  • Information about your organization and filing operations provided during consultations
  • Usage data from the D.A. BluePrint platform related to workflow and filing activities
  • Technical information such as browser type and IP address for security and performance purposes

How We Use Information

Information collected is used to:

  • Respond to inquiries and schedule consultations
  • Deliver and improve D.A. BluePrint Technologies products and services
  • Communicate updates about our platform relevant to your use
  • Ensure the security and reliability of our systems

Information Sharing

D.A. BluePrint Technologies does not sell or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes. We may share information with service providers who assist in delivering our platform, subject to confidentiality obligations. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our clients and systems.

Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we encourage you to contact us if you have security concerns.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us at hello@da-blueprint.com.
