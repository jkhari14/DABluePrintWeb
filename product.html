Platform Architecture

Connected workflow intelligence from intake to submission.

The D.A. BluePrint platform connects project intake, pre-planning analysis, document review, and submission guidance so teams can prepare and file with greater accuracy, efficiency, and operational clarity.

01 Project Intake

Capture project details, job type, team assignments, and documentation inventory to establish a structured filing baseline from day one.

02 Pre-Planning

Identify filing risks, coordination requirements, and documentation gaps before submission preparation begins — reducing rework and downstream delays.

03 Validation

Review job information against workflow logic and filing requirements to surface gaps, risks, and coordination issues before DOB review.

04 Submission

Receive step-by-step submission guidance, track readiness across outstanding items, and coordinate the final filing handoff with confidence.
