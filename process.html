How It Works

A structured workflow for every NYC filing engagement.

The D.A. BluePrint process is designed to reduce filing preparation inefficiencies, surface submission risks early, and provide teams with structured guidance at every critical stage of the filing workflow.

01

Project Intake

Capture project details, filing type, documentation inventory, job information, and initial team requirements to establish a structured baseline for the engagement.

02

Pre-Planning Review

Identify potential filing issues, documentation gaps, and coordination requirements before submission preparation begins. Reduce surprises and improve team readiness.

03

Filing Validation

Review job information against workflow logic and NYC filing requirements. Surface outstanding items and submission risks before they become delays or rejection issues.

04

Submission Guidance

Receive structured next steps, outstanding item checklists, and submission readiness guidance to reduce errors, rework cycles, and filing delays.
